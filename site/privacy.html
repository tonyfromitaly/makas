Privacy policy

This page explains what {{name}} collects on this website and in the {{product}} app, why, and what you can do about it. A full policy will replace it when the company is incorporated; company details will be added here then.

Who we are

{{product}} is a product of {{name}}. Questions about privacy: {{email}}.

What this website collects

When you request a demo, we collect your name, your business’s name and type, your email and, if you give them, your phone number, city and message. We use them only to answer your request, talk with you and, if you choose, set up {{product}} with you.

The website sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting provider’s traffic analytics are blocked on these pages.

What the app holds

A business that uses {{product}} keeps its own records in it: appointments, client names and phone numbers, sales, and its books. Those records belong to that business. We process them on the business’s behalf and keep each business’s data separate, in the app and in the database.

  • Text reminders go only to clients who opted in. Every message says how to opt out: reply STOP.
  • The assistant, Mona, keeps conversations per user so it can follow context. Users can clear them at any time; otherwise they are deleted after 90 days.
  • Card payments, when they open, are handled by the payment processor. Card numbers never reach our systems.

We don’t sell your data

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we don’t add you to marketing lists.

Service providers

We use these providers to run the service, under contracts that limit them to that purpose:

  • Cloudflare: website hosting and DNS.
  • Render: app server and database, in the United States (Virginia).
  • Resend: email delivery.
  • Anthropic: the AI model behind the assistant. It receives the sentence a user types or says and the context needed to answer it.

How long we keep it

  • Demo requests: deleted within 12 months after our conversation ends.
  • App data: kept while the subscription is active, and deleted 90 days after it ends.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email {{email}} and we’ll reply within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for asking. Business owners can also download all their data as one file, or delete their account, from Settings in the app.

If you are a client of a business that uses {{product}}, that business decides what it records about you. Contact the business first; we will help it answer your request.

Children

{{product}} is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this page, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change matters to how we use your information, we’ll tell business owners by email first.
