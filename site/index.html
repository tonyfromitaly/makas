      Security & privacy

      Your business’s data is yours.

      • Every business in its own vault

        Each business’s records are kept apart, and the separation is enforced twice: in the app and in the database.

      • Passwords kept as hashes

        Passwords are stored with argon2id, not as plain text. A sign-in key works once, and resetting your password with one signs out your other devices.

      • No texts without consent

        Clients get reminders only if they opted in, and every message tells them how to reply STOP.

      • Export or delete, any time

        Download your clients, visits, sales and books as one file, or delete your account for good.

      • Numbers from your own books

        Totals like income, expenses and what’s due are computed from your records. When Mona answers in her own words, check the figures in Books.

      • Card numbers never reach us

        When payments open, cards are handled by the payment processor; card numbers never touch our systems.
