# Fade Privacy Policy (United States)

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains how [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") handles personal information in connection with Fade. It covers three groups of people:

1. **Business customers:** owners and staff who use Fade.
2. **Clients of those businesses:** people who book an appointment or receive a reminder.
3. **Website visitors.**

For client data, the business is in charge and we process it for them. See section 5.

## 1. What we collect

| From | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| Business owners and staff | Name, e-mail, password (stored only as a one-way hash), role, business name and address, time zone, language | You give it to us when you sign up or are invited |
| Business records | Appointments, services, sales, tips, books of income and expenses, staff pay settings, notes | Entered by the business or by its staff |
| Connected services (at the business's request) | **Square:** payments, customers and bookings. **Plaid:** bank transactions and balances for the accounts the business links | Read through those services' official connections |
| Clients of a business | Name, phone number, preferred language and channel, reminder consent and its date and time, appointment history, and a short note the business may add | From the business, or from the client through the online booking page |
| Messages | Reminder content, delivery status, STOP replies | Our messaging providers |
| Assistant | What users type or say to the assistant, and the short notes it keeps | From the user. Speech is converted to text in the user's browser; audio is not sent to us |
| Website visitors | What you enter in the demo or application form | You give it to us. The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies |
| Technical | IP address, browser type, request logs, error logs | Automatically, for security and to keep the service running |

We do not collect Social Security numbers, card numbers or bank login credentials. Bank linking happens inside Plaid; we never see the bank password.

## 2. How we use it

We use personal information to:

- provide the Service, including appointments, reminders, the till, reports, connections and the assistant;
- secure the Service, prevent abuse and debug problems;
- support and communicate with business customers, including account and billing e-mails;
- comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do **not**:

- sell personal information;
- share it for cross-context behavioral advertising;
- use one business's data to serve or advertise to another.

## 5. Clients of businesses that use Fade

If you booked with, or received a reminder from, a business that uses Fade:

- That business decides what is collected and how it is used.
- We process your information on its behalf.
- Requests about your data should go to the business first. We will help the business respond.

**Reminders:**

- You receive reminders only if you agreed, for example by ticking the reminder box when booking.
- Reply **STOP** to any reminder to stop them.
- Reply **HELP** for help.
- Message and data rates may apply. See `sms-terms.md`.

## 7. Security

- Connection tokens are stored encrypted.
- Each business's data is separated at the database level.
- Access to client phone numbers is logged.
- No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and authorities as the law requires.
